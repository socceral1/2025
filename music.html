Music

I listen to a lot of genres but my favorite one is K-pop. K-pop is Korean pop and it consists of many girl and boy bands.



Some of my favorite bands...



Stray Kids

Stray Kids is a 8 member boy band that debuted in 2018 under JYP Entertainment. They released many songs and are popular worldwide!

Itzy

Itzy is a 5 member girl group that debuted in 2019 under JYP. This girl group is famous for their energetic performances on stage.

Aespa

Aespa is a 4 member girl group debuted in 2020 under SM Entertainment. They have many songs including Black Mamba their most viewed music video.



Blackpink

Blackpink is a 4 girl group debuted in 2016 under YG Entertainment. They are very popular and you won't miss them on the big screen in Times Square.



BTS

BTS is a 7 memebr boy band debuted in 2013 under HYBE Labels. BTS is worldwide known and famous for their song Butter.



Babymonster

Babymonster is a 7 member girl group debuted 2024 under YG Entertainment. They release very good songs that you can vibe to.



Allday Project

Allday Project is a co-ed K-pop group which is very rare. They consist of 5 members, 3 girls and 2 boys. They debuted a few weeks ago and is under The Black Label.
